Hyphenation patterns bundled in PhotoCraft (Capishot) paragraph-text hyphenation
=================================================================================

The English, Spanish and Portuguese Liang hyphenation patterns of the TeX hyph-utf8 package
(https://github.com/hyphenation/tex-hyphen, http://www.hyphenation.org/tex) are embedded in the
binary as compiled automata by the `hypher` crate (https://github.com/typst/hypher, MIT OR
Apache-2.0, by Laurenz Mädje and contributors), built with only its `english`, `spanish` and
`portuguese` features. Their copyright notices and licences follow, as the licences require.
